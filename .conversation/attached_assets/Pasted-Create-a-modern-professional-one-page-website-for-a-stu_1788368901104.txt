Create a modern, professional one-page website for a student AI healthcare project called “Badira | بادرة”.

Badira is an AI Early Awareness Companion for Preeclampsia. The website is a project landing page for a university AI hackathon, not the actual clinical application.

Build it as a responsive website that works well on desktop and mobile.

The page should contain these sections in this order:

1. Navbar with “Badira | بادرة” and links to About, How It Works, Features, Team, and Contact.

2. Hero section:
- Badira | بادرة
- “An AI Early Awareness Companion for Preeclampsia”
- Main tagline: “Know earlier. Act at the right time.”
- Highlight the concept: “Risk × Reliability × Time”
- A “Learn How It Works” button that scrolls to the How It Works section.

3. The Problem:
Explain that preeclampsia is a serious pregnancy complication where early risk identification matters, but women may not always recognize when their medical history, pregnancy factors, or available measurements suggest a need for earlier professional assessment.
Highlight: “The challenge is not only identifying risk — it is recognizing it early enough to act.”

4. The Solution:
Introduce Badira as a bilingual, mobile-first AI companion designed to support early awareness of preeclampsia. Explain that it uses information a woman can readily provide, such as gestational week, medical history, previous pregnancies, family history, and available measurements.

5. Risk × Reliability × Time:
Create three visually prominent cards:
- Risk: What does the available information suggest?
- Reliability: How reliable is the assessment based on the completeness and consistency of the available information?
- Time: Based on gestational stage, how promptly may professional assessment be appropriate?

6. How It Works:
Show four steps:
- Tell Badira about your pregnancy
- Add what you know
- AI evaluates the information
- Understand the result

7. Key Features:
Include six feature cards:
- Arabic + English
- AI-Powered Awareness
- Reliability-Aware
- Time-Aware
- Explainable Results
- Shareable Summary

8. Who It Is For:
Pregnant women seeking a simple and accessible way to understand whether their pregnancy and health information may warrant earlier professional assessment for preeclampsia.

9. About the Project:
Explain that Badira grew from research exploring trustworthy AI in women’s health and was transformed into a focused hackathon concept for accessible early preeclampsia awareness.

10. Team section:
Create placeholder cards for team members. Do not invent names or roles. Use “Team Member” and “Role” placeholders that can be edited later.

11. Medical disclaimer:
Make this clearly visible:
“Badira is not a diagnostic or treatment system and does not replace professional medical care.”
Explain that it supports early awareness and informed conversations with healthcare professionals and does not provide diagnosis or replace clinical screening.

12. Contact / footer:
Include “Earlier awareness. Better conversations. At the right time.”
Include Badira | بادرة and “Risk × Reliability × Time”.
Use placeholder contact information for now.

DESIGN:
Use a sophisticated women’s-health + technology aesthetic, not a generic hospital website and not a childish pregnancy website.

Use:
- warm off-white backgrounds
- deep plum as the primary color
- muted dusty rose accents
- subtle sage accents
- dark charcoal text

The design should feel calm, trustworthy, premium, modern, and medically responsible.

Use elegant typography, generous whitespace, rounded cards, subtle shadows, simple healthcare/technology-inspired graphics or abstract shapes, and tasteful animations.

Do not make the website overly pink.
Do not use cliché baby graphics.
Do not make unsupported medical claims.
Do not create a working risk assessment or fake AI prediction.
